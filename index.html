Research

Research themes

My work combines physical modeling, field measurements and machine learning to address practical reliability and condition-monitoring problems in electrical power systems.

01 / GROUNDING

Grounding systems & condition monitoring

Measurement, modeling and online assessment of grounding systems in wind farms, transmission lines and multi-grounded electrical installations.

02 / MEASUREMENT

Electrical measurement & instrumentation

Methods for energized-condition measurements, surface-potential sensing, clamp-on techniques, residual-current monitoring and practical field validation.

03 / DATA

Machine learning for electrical diagnosis

Data-driven regression and classification for fault diagnosis, anomaly identification and estimation of difficult-to-measure electrical quantities.

04 / PHYSICS

Computational electromagnetics & EMC

Finite-element and reduced-order modeling, electromagnetic compatibility, power electronics and the translation of physical models into deployable monitoring methods.
